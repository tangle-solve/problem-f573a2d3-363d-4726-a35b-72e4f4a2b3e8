### CHEM-000432: A bottleable triplet phosphinidene that avoids intramolecular arene addition
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Isolate a free, monocoordinate triplet phosphinidene in a hydrindacene-type environment while suppressing reaction with its own aryl framework. Establish its spin state, identity, and solution persistence.
- **Status:** The 2024 attempt instead produced singlet phosphanorcaradiene through intramolecular dearomatization. This failed triplet isolation is still discussed in 2026 primary literature; isolated singlet phosphinidenes and precursor equivalents do not fulfill this specific target.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Exposes phosphorus-centered triplet chemistry without precursor-release equilibria.
- **Key works:** Y. Chen, P. Su, D. Wang, Z. Ke, and G. Tan (2024), “Molecular-strain induced phosphinidene reactivity of a phosphanorcaradiene,” [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-49042-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49042-1).
- **Related:** Spin physics—stabilizing reactive high-spin molecular states.
